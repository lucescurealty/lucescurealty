Institutional Services

Institutional Services "IS" is our founding platform that spans five decades of best in class services. Team members have transacted on some of the most noteworthy properties in the Western United States on behalf of many of the largest investors in the world. Many markets, including major MSA's throughout Southern and Northern California, Las Vegas, Phoenix, Tucson, Salt Lake City and other western US markets have synergies in ownership, tenancies, and market characteristics and IS teams on the ground work together on single assets and portfolio assignments in a seamless, uniform, manner without competing with each other.

  • Financial Underwriting | Modeling
  • Market Analytics

Capital Markets Services

Lucescu Capital Markets Services provides equity and debt placement services on disposition assignments and/or 3rd party transactions. The Capital Markets team has access to the same sales, financial underwriting and market analytics teams as our other CORE Services.

Led by an entrepreneurial executive team, our highly-crafted and strategic financing solutions help clients meet their unique investment objectives and achieve a timely close.
