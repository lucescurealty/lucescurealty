About Lucescu Realty

Lucescu Realty is a market leading investment real estate services firm that uniquely combines major capital markets expertise with a complete and thorough understanding of real estate fundamentals to execute a wide range of services on behalf of our clients.

Our company was founded, and continues to aspire, to provide our clients with best in class, actionable intelligence for optimal executable solutions in investment real estate, regardless of location, or property type.

Story

Lucescu Realty specializes in the acquisition, disposition, financing, and management of real estate properties throughout the Western United States. Lucescu Realty was previously part of one of the largest national commercial real estate brokerage firms for nearly 20 years. During that time, the Lucescu Group (Lucescu Realty's predecessor) was consistently the number one retail investment sales group nationwide and routinely accounted for the majority of the firm's most significant and noteworthy retail and office sales transactions.


Lucescu's expertise and track record was directly responsible for the roll out of a national retail group in multi tenant and single tenant, NNN, transactions. Building from a strong and successful performance history, Lucescu Realty continues to be annually recognized as one of the most active brokerage firms in the United States in the sale of retail shopping centers. We are headquartered on Newport Center Drive in Newport Beach, California and maintain regional offices in Las Vegas, NV and Phoenix, AZ.
